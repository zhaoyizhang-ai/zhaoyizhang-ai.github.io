> **Example content — replace or delete before publishing.** This note is not a statement about completed study or work.

Notes are intentionally lighter than blog posts. Use them for concise learning records, references, or small observations.

## Suggested structure

- State the question or topic.
- Record the useful idea in your own words.
- Add links or citations where appropriate.
- Note what remains uncertain.
